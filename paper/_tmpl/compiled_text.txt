Abstract
Nine percent of this dataset is labelled. That is the condition self-supervised1
learning exists for, and it was what we set out to do: pretrain on the unlabelled2
remainder, fine-tune on the little that is annotated. But a pretext task cannot be3
evaluated without a supervised baseline to beat, and this dataset supplies neither a4
baseline nor the negative class one would need, because no nurse ever recorded5
being unstressed. All 358 survey entries mark an episode a nurse flagged; the6
number attached is its severity, not its presence. This paper is the attrition process7
required to construct both, reported as the result. We start from 609 recordings and8
15 participants and arrive at 138 episodes from 10, testing at each step whether9
what survived is real rather than manufactured. The labels do mark a physiological10
state, but in one channel only: skin conductance rises at reported onset in64.3%11
of episodes (p= 0.0009 ), while heart rate rises in48.6%, which is what a coin12
flip produces (p= 0.80 ). The constructed comparison group survives falsification,13
detecting neither movement nor shift schedule (both AUC 0.51), and the baseline14
built on it clears a within-participant permutation null decisively ( 0.765 against15
0.498±0.010 ) while still recovering only10.6%of episodes at one false alarm16
per worn hour. We then ran the pretraining anyway. Over 1,238 unlabelled hours it17
matches random initialisation at every label count from 25 to 138 episodes, and18
both trail the hand-crafted baseline by 0.11 AUC. Severity prediction, the one task19
here needing no constructed negatives, is also at chance. The constraint is the label20
supply, and no amount of unlabelled data substitutes for it.21
1 Introduction22
Nurses leave the profession at rates that track measured occupational stress, and stressed clinicians23
make more errors. The case for measuring stress continuously is therefore both an occupational-health24
and a patient-safety argument. The instrument normally used, a retrospective questionnaire, is a poor25
one: it is administered after the fact, it depends on recall, and it cannot resolve when during a shift26
the stress occurred.27
Wrist-worn sensors promise to fix that. Skin conductance, heart rate, skin temperature, and movement28
can be recorded continuously and unobtrusively for a week at a time, and skin conductance in29
particular is driven almost entirely by the sympathetic nervous system with little voluntary control,30
which is why it has long been the channel of choice for laboratory work on arousal. Moving that31
measurement from the laboratory onto a hospital ward changes the problem in ways that are easy32
to underestimate. The wrist is in constant motion, and the same physical exertion that raises heart33
rate and sweating during a stressful episode raises them during an unremarkable one. Ground truth34
stops being an experimenter-controlled stimulus and becomes whatever the participant remembers35

to report, when she has a moment to report it. A public dataset now exists for testing the idea.36
Hosseini et al. ( 2) recorded 15 nurses for one week each during the COVID-19 outbreak, with37
periodic smartphone-administered stress reports alongside the sensor streams.38
What we set out to do.Only9.1%of the 1,251.7 recorded hours falls inside a reported episode.39
The other90.9%is unlabelled physiology, which is precisely the regime self-supervised learning was40
developed for: learn the structure of ordinary physiology from everything, then fine-tune on the little41
that is annotated. Our intent was that experiment.42
What stopped us.Two prerequisites, neither of which the dataset supplies. A pretext task is only43
meaningful against a supervised baseline worth improving on, and no such baseline existed for this44
dataset that we could trust. Building one requires a negative class, and there is none: every survey45
row marks an episode a participant flagged, so the annotation records severity, not presence. The46
comparison group has to be constructed from unlabelled time, and constructing it defensibly turned47
out to be the entire study.48
Why the groundwork is the contribution.Published results on this dataset are strong. Mathur49
et al. (4) report a weighted F1 of 0.99 for two-level classification. We do not reproduce anything50
close to it, and the reason we think it is worth reporting our own attrition in full is that each step of51
that attrition is a place where a number can be inflated without anyone noticing: adjacent analysis52
windows are near-duplicates of one another, the negative class is invented, and the effective sample53
size is 138 episodes from 10 people rather than the 24,334 windows those episodes generate.54
We therefore report the study as a sequence of eliminations. Does the label mark anything physiologi-55
cal (Section 3)? Does the constructed comparison group leak a confound (Section 4)? How far do56
standard features and a standard learner get, and can the pipeline manufacture its own signal (Section57
5)? Does a different treatment of the labels help (Section 6)? Does a learned representation (Section58
7)? Each answer narrows what is left, and what is left at the end is the data.59
Contributions.An onset analysis showing that one of four channels responds and the others do not,60
which reorders the feature priorities for this sensor. A negative-class construction with falsification61
probes, and the finding that careful constructionimprovesrather than inflates performance, against the62
usual warning. A decision rule fixed before results were seen, and the measurement that answered it.63
And a label-efficiency sweep establishing that pretraining over 1,238 unlabelled hours buys nothing64
here, reported with the comparison biased in its favour.65
2 Data, units, and what survives66
We use the original Dryad deposit rather than the merged files in common circulation. The merged67
version resamples every channel onto a shared grid, which discards the blood volume pulse and68
inter-beat interval streams and so removes the only route to heart rate variability. Channels are69
read at their native rates from the file headers: acceleration 32 Hz, blood volume pulse 64 Hz, skin70
conductance and temperature 4 Hz, heart rate 1 Hz. Published descriptions giving 72 Hz and 10 Hz71
for pulse and temperature disagree with the files, and we follow the files. The archive holds 60972
sessions over 1,251.7 hours and 250 person-days; the median session is 1.17 hours rather than a shift,73
so a participant’s week arrives as roughly 40 fragments.74
Two clocks.Sensor timestamps are UTC and survey timestamps are bare local wall-clock with no75
zone recorded. Choosing wrongly places nearly every episode outside its own recording and raises76
no error, so we resolved it by counting how many rated episodes start inside a session belonging to77
the same participant. As UTC, 50 of 245 (20.4%); as daylight-saving-aware local time, 212 (86.5%).78
Thirty-three rated episodes fall outside every session even then and are set aside.79
The unit of prediction.A 120 s window with a 60 s hop. No labelled episode is shorter than 120 s80
and a 300 s window would discard 15 outright; independently, ultra-short heart rate variability work81
finds 120 s sufficient for time-domain measures (5), with successive-difference measures validated82
from 15 s (6). A window ispositiveif at least half of it falls inside a high-severity episode,negative83
only if it survives Section 4, and otherwiseexcludedrather than set to zero.84

3 Do the labels mark anything physiological?105
Before building a classifier, we ask whether there is anything to classify. We align every episode at106
its reported onset, normalise each channel against the participant’s own preceding hour, and compare107
the ten minutes after onset against the thirty before.108
3
Skin conductance rises in 64.3% of episodes, surviving a sign test and a Wilcoxon signed-rank test109
(p= 3.3×10 −6), and the raw median shift of 0.64µ S sits on a 0.68µ S baseline, roughly a doubling.110
Heart rate rises in 48.6%, which is what a coin flip produces.111
Why the mean is unusable.The pooled mean reports a skin conductance shift of +9.75 normalised112
units, of which 63% comes from five episodes out of 140. The cause is numerical rather than113
physiological: the trailing interquartile range in the denominator approaches zero on flat stretches,114
and the largest single value reached +1600 where a plausible score is near ±3. We bound the115
denominator at its empirical first percentile, clip the output, and fix the use of medians and sign tests116
in advance, because the same data yields opposite headlines otherwise. This analysis is descriptive117
and selects no model.118
The result reorders everything after it. The problem is well posed, and well posed in one channel; any119
later result driven by heart rate deserves suspicion.120
4 Constructing a comparison group121
4.1 Eligibility122
A candidate negative window must lie in a session of at least thirty minutes, fall on a participant-day123
containing at least one reported episode, sit at least thirty minutes from the boundary of any reported124
episode including the unrated ones, and not itself be positive.125
The second condition carries the argument. On a day a participant filed nothing, silence is uninfor-126
mative: she may have been calm, or too busy to open the application. On a day she filed at least127
one report, she was demonstrably engaging with the survey, so silence through the rest of that day128
is evidence rather than absence of evidence. It is also the most expensive condition, cutting 24,334129
windows to 16,214. All four together leave 7,955 candidates, or 265.2 hours.130
4.2 Matching rather than filtering131
Nurses move more when busy and busy is stressful, so movement tracks stress without being a132
stress signal. Choosing quiet periods as negatives would produce two classes differing in both stress133
and movement, and a classifier would separate them by detecting movement. We therefore bin the134
positive class into deciles of mean acceleration and draw negatives from the same binwithin the135
same participant. Matching globally would draw disproportionately from high-coverage participants,136
making participant identity a partial label proxy along exactly the axis the evaluation holds out.137
4.3 The ratio, and why it is uniform138
Class balance determines where the decision threshold belongs. A fixed model at recall 0.80 and139
specificity 0.80 scoresF1 = 0.727 in a fold that is one third positive andF1 = 0.195 in one that is140
one thirtieth positive, so per-fold metrics would not be comparable if balance varied. Exact uniformity141
is unreachable: the binding constraint is one participant’s highest-activity bin holding 13 positives142
against 7 candidates, so matching everyone exactly would need a ratio below one. We fix1.31, which143
halves the residual spread from 1.64× to 1.28×, report a four-arm sensitivity analysis in Table 3, and144
note that the2.14arm scores marginally higher AUC while leaving balance more uneven.145
Negatives are drawn under three seeds. The seed-to-seed spread is the noise floor, and any later146
comparison smaller than it is not a comparison.147
4.4 Falsification148
We then build models we want to fail, each given only one suspicious signal and the same learner as149
the baseline, so a null is attributable to absence of signal rather than to a weaker model.150
A model given only acceleration reaches 0.513 and one given only the hour of day reaches 0.507,151
against chance 0.500, in all four arms. The comparison group leaks neither movement nor schedule.152
Participant identity is recoverable from the same features at 0.768 against chance 0.100, which we153
record as a measurement rather than a failure, since physiology genuinely differs between people; it154
is the reference any learned representation must later be compared against.155

5 Baseline156
Features are extracted at native rates and then aggregated, because detecting skin conductance157
responses on a 1 Hz downsample loses roughly 40% of them. The set covers tonic level and slope,158
phasic variability, response count and amplitude for skin conductance; mean, variability, slope,159
maximum and deviation from a trailing median for heart rate; magnitude statistics and a movement160
fraction for acceleration; slope and trailing deviation for temperature, never its absolute value, whose161
intraclass correlation with participant identity is0.52; and hour of day with position in session.162
Heart rate variability is excluded. The inter-beat interval file is present in every session, but only163
5.9% of 120 s windows contain a run of clean consecutive beats long enough to use, and the archive164
holds 39.5 usable hours out of 1,251.7. A commonly reported session-level availability of 48% is165
eight times more flattering than the window-level figure that actually governs a per-window feature.166
Continuous channels are normalised against a trailing sixty-minute robust baseline within session,167
which uses only past data and is therefore both leakage-free and reproducible at deployment. The168
model is gradient boosting with balanced class weights, evaluated leave-one-participant-out across169
three seeds.170
The model attains AUC0.7654with a seed range of0.0095.171
The gap is the finding.An AUC of 0.765 means the model reliably ranks stressed windows above172
unstressed ones. At one false alarm per worn hour it recovers 10.6% of episodes, with a seed range173

would tolerate; constrained to the usable corner, little remains. Two of ten participants detect zero175
episodes at any usable threshold and one is below chance at 0.388. We report per fold and never176
average; fold AUC spans0.388to0.885.177

6 Does a different treatment of the labels help?183
We fixed a rule before computing the following: proceed to representation learning only if a different184
label treatment moves episode recall at one alarm per hour by more than the baseline seed range of185
0.0724. The threshold is the baseline’s own measured spread rather than a chosen constant, which186
makes the rule generous in one direction, since a noisier baseline sets a higher bar.187
Three treatments on identical folds, windows and features: the curated negatives of Section 4; a naive188
comparator calling all unlabelled time negative, with no same-day rule, guard band, or matching; and189
non-negative positive-unlabelled risk estimation (3) fitted participant-stratified, with the class prior190
bracketed rather than estimated since it is not identifiable from positive-unlabelled data (1).191
The threshold to clear was0.1787; the best alternative reached0.0652, a change of−0.0411.The rule192
was not met, and every alternative is worse than the baseline rather than merely insufficiently193
better.194
Two things are worth separating out.Curation improved rather than inflated: this comparison195
normally detects inflation, because naive negatives include off-shift and sleeping time and the contrast196
becomes trivial, yet the curated construction beats naive by 0.073 AUC. The likely mechanism is197
the guard band, since naive negatives include windows adjacent to episodes that are physiologically198
similar to positives and act as label noise; we have not isolated the guard band from the same-day199
rule, so we offer this as plausible rather than demonstrated. Andthe prior does not rescue it: AUC200
rises monotonically from 0.662 to 0.717 across the grid, which is the shape one could present as201
promising, while episode recall stays between0.044and0.065throughout.202
7 Does a learned representation help?203
The rule above tested how the labels areused. Self-supervision asks whether a differentrepresentation204
helps, which is a different axis, so we ran it past our own stopping point.205
A dilated 1D convolutional encoder is trained by masked reconstruction over all 609 sessions and all206
15 participants, including the five outside the supervised cohort, since the pretext task needs no labels.207
The corpus is 1,238 usable hours at 1 Hz over five causally normalised channels, giving 68k windows.208
Mask spans of 30 to 120 s are fixed a priori rather than tuned on downstream performance, which209
would leak through the selection process. Contrastive learning was rejected deliberately: its standard210
augmentations include amplitude scaling, and skin conductance amplitude is the signal Section 3211
identified.212
The identity gate.A linear probe on frozen embeddings recovers participant identity at 0.168,213
against chance 0.067 and the 0.768 reachable from hand-crafted features. The encoder did not learn214
identity, so the phase proceeds. Whether 0.168 instead indicates a weak representation is settled by215
the sweep.216

8 Discussion233
Six eliminations, in the order we ran them. The labels mark a real physiological state, in one channel.234
The comparison group leaks neither movement nor schedule. The pipeline does not manufacture235
signal, since a within-participant permutation null sits at chance. Model capacity is not the constraint,236
since a linear model matches a boosted one. Three treatments of the labels do not improve on the237
baseline and two make it worse. A learned representation over 1,238 unlabelled hours does not238
improve on it either, and neither does severity, which needs no constructed negatives.239
What remains is the data. There are 138 episodes from 10 participants. The labels are retrospective240
self-reports of intervals, treated here as uniform states although a thirty-minute report is unlikely to241
describe thirty uniform minutes. The negative class is constructed rather than observed. One of four242
channels responds at onset.243
This bears on the publishedF1 of 0.99 (4). We do not reproduce it and think it is difficult to reconcile244
with participant-grouped evaluation, since adjacent windows share raw samples and participant-245
specific baselines, so a split that ignores participant boundaries places near-duplicates on both sides.246
We are not able to diagnose another group’s protocol from a published summary and note the247
discrepancy without attributing a cause, reporting our own protocol in full so the comparison can be248
made.249
The actionable consequence concerns label supply rather than modelling. A collection targeting this250
task should record explicit non-stress intervals, since their absence makes every downstream number251
conditional on a construction; capture onset more finely than a retrospective interval; and prioritise252
skin conductance quality, since four of fifteen participants here produced a flat trace on the only253
responsive channel. The general form of the lesson is that self-supervision does not manufacture the254
thing this dataset lacks. Unlabelled data at a ratio of ten to one buys nothing when what is missing is255
not signal volume but the annotation that makes signal interpretable.256

9 Limitations257
The cohort is 10 of 15 female nurses at a single hospital during a pandemic, so external validity is258
narrow. Negatives are constructed, so precision is not reported as a performance claim: when the259
model flags an unlabelled window we cannot distinguish a false alarm from an unreported episode.260
The class prior is not identifiable and is bracketed rather than estimated. Our positive-unlabelled261
implementation uses a fixed training budget without an inner validation split, so its negative result262
is weaker evidence than the naive comparator, which shares the baseline’s learner and features and263
differs only in label treatment. We searched two learners and one hand-crafted feature set. Thirty-264
three rated episodes have no sensor coverage and are absent from every metric. Two normalisation265
constants, the denominator floor and the output clip, are choices we record and vary rather than defend.266
Ten folds is underpowered for the paired comparisons we report, and we state spreads descriptively267
rather than dividing by the square root of the fold count. The permutation null uses twenty draws, so268
itsp-value floor is0.048.269
The pretraining result carries its own qualifications. The encoder saw every participant, so Table 6270
reports transductive upper bounds rather than estimates for an unseen person; per-fold pretraining is271
the clean alternative and costs ten times the compute. One architecture and one training budget were272
tried. A representation that is weak and one genuinely free of participant identity produce the same273
low probe accuracy, and we separate them only indirectly, through the sweep. The severity task rests274
on 17 medium-severity episodes, which is why we report cumulative links and a collapsed task rather275
than a three-class model.276
10 Conclusion277
We intended to test whether self-supervised pretraining helps when 90.9% of the data is unlabelled.278
Establishing the prerequisites consumed the study, and the prerequisites turned out to be the result.279
The labels mark a real state in one channel. A comparison group can be built that survives falsification.280
A baseline built on it clears a permutation null decisively and still recovers one episode in ten at a281
deployable alarm rate. Neither a different learner, nor three different treatments of the labels, nor282
pretraining over 1,238 unlabelled hours moves it, and severity, which needs no constructed negatives,283
is at chance as well.284
What a future study most needs from this one is not a score but a measurement of what was missing,285
and the answer is labels: explicit non-stress intervals, finer onset resolution, and skin conductance286
quality good enough that a participant’s only responsive channel is not flat.287
